A2–B1 ENGLISH
👉
1 • ACTION

Imperative Sentences

We use imperative sentences to give instructions. Start with Verb 1. We do not need a subject like “you”.

Positive: Verb 1 + object

Negative: Don't + Verb 1
Positive: Tap the filter icon.
Negative: Don't share your password.
🔢
2 • ORDER

Sequence Connectors

These words show the order of actions, from the first step to the last step.

Beginning: First / Firstly
Middle: Next / Then / After that
Ending: Finally / Lastly

Example: First, open the app. Next, tap “Scan”. Finally, check the payment.

🌟
3 • HOW

Adverbs of Manner

Adverbs of manner tell us how to do an action. Many adverbs end in -ly.

careful → carefully
gentle → gently
thorough → thoroughly
proper → properly
Special: good → well; fast → fast; hard → hard.

“Stir gently” gives a clear instruction. It is better than “stir violently”.

⚠️
4 • SAFETY

Modals and Warnings

Use these expressions to give rules, advice, and warnings.

You must turn off the engine. (obligation)
You should wait for two minutes. (advice)
Be careful not to press the button twice. (warning)
Make sure to close the cap tightly. (caution)

💡 Mindful Learning: Choose precise words

Compare these instructions:

“Stir gently.” → The mixture stays in the bowl.
“Stir violently.” → The hot drink may spill.

Clear words help people follow instructions correctly and safely.
